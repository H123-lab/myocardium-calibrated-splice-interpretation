## Status

RECONSTRUCTION DOCUMENTATION ONLY.

The original executable variant-annotation pipeline is not currently available.

## Variant scope

The study evaluated TTN and MYH7 variants relevant to splicing, including:

- canonical splice-site variants;
- non-canonical splice-region variants;
- TTN truncating variants;
- splice-proximal MYH7 variants.

## Reported annotation fields

The manuscript reports annotation using:

- gene;
- transcript;
- genome build;
- genomic coordinate;
- cDNA change;
- protein consequence where applicable;
- exon/intron;
- variant type;
- gnomAD allele frequency;
- structural domain;
- prior clinical classification;
- transcriptomic evidence;
- AI-derived prediction;
- comparator prediction.

## Interpretation

Variant interpretation was described as ACMG/AMP-aligned research analysis incorporating transcriptomic and computational evidence.

The framework-derived classification should not be represented as an independently validated clinical diagnostic classification.

## Missing historical artifacts
