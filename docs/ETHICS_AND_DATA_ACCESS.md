# Ethics and Data Access

## Study design

This study was a secondary analysis of previously generated datasets.

No new participants were recruited and no new biological samples were collected for the present analysis.

## Original studies

The original contributing studies were responsible for obtaining the ethical approvals, consent procedures, and data-sharing permissions applicable to their respective data collections.

The relevant source publications and repository records should be consulted for the governing ethical framework of each dataset.

## Present secondary analysis

The author did not obtain a separate participant-level prospective recruitment or intervention approval because the study was performed as a secondary analysis of previously generated datasets.

However, the exact institutional determination governing the secondary analysis is not currently documented.

Accordingly, this repository does not claim a specific exemption, waiver, IRB approval, or institutional determination that cannot be supported by documentary evidence.

## Public resources

Public datasets were accessed through the terms established by their originating repositories.

Examples include NCBI GEO and SRA resources.

## Controlled-access resources

The author recalls accessing at least one controlled-access resource but the exact repository, authorization mechanism, and access record are no longer available.

This item remains unresolved and must not be described as having a specific authorization number until documentary evidence is recovered.

## Participant-level data

Participant-level raw sequencing data are not redistributed through this repository.

Users should obtain source data directly from the originating repositories and comply with their respective data-use conditions.

## Ethical reproducibility statement

The repository is intended to document data provenance and access conditions without disclosing protected participant information or claiming ethical approvals that cannot be verified.
